Bio

I've been filming since I was a kid — always the one with a camera on every trip, every gathering, every ordinary afternoon that felt worth keeping. That habit turned into a craft, and the craft is now something I bring to other people's projects.

I work across video and photo — commercials, brand content, events, portraits, whatever the story needs. Based in Copenhagen, currently studying software at AAU, filming everything else in between.
